Privacy Policy

How Victor’s Mexican Food handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 2026

Victor’s Mexican Food respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit this website, contact the restaurant, use links to third-party platforms, or place an order through online ordering tools.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, order details, or messages submitted through website forms or ordering features.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, process orders, improve the website experience, and communicate with guests when needed.

Third-Party Services

This website may link to or embed services such as Clover, Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram, Yelp, and other platforms. Those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Cookies and Analytics

The website may use standard browser technologies, analytics, or embedded platform tools to understand site performance and improve the guest experience.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to Victor’s Mexican Food at (760) 341-3553.
