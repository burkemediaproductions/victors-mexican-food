No cans. No bottles. No shortcuts. Just thoughtful cooking, made with fresh ingredients and care. From salsas and sauces to our classic tacos, burritos, and combination plates, the menu is built around flavor you can taste.

Heritage and hometown

From Jalisco & Zacatecas to Palm Desert.

Victor’s story traces back to a rural village in Zacatecas & Jalisco, where his grandfather ran a small store along the old Camino Real route. That spirit of resilience, service, and family ambition still shapes the restaurant today.
