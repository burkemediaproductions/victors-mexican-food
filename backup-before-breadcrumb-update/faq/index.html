FAQ

Helpful answers before you visit.

Find quick answers about ordering, dine-in, takeout, hours, location, and menu questions at Victor’s Mexican Food.

Good to know

Planning your visit is easy.

Whether you are dining in, picking up takeout, or browsing the menu online, Victor’s keeps the experience simple and welcoming.

Browse MenuGet Directions
Do you offer dine-in and takeout?

Yes. Victor’s Mexican Food welcomes dine-in guests and offers takeout for guests on the go.

Can I order online?

Yes. You can browse the live menu and order online when online ordering is available. When online ordering is closed, the menu remains available to browse.

Where are you located?

Victor’s Mexican Food is located at 74600 CA-111 Suite F, Palm Desert, CA 92260.

Do you serve breakfast?

Yes. Breakfast burritos and breakfast plates are available during morning hours.

What kinds of food are guests known to love?

Guests often mention tacos, burritos, fish tacos, chile verde, carnitas, chorizo breakfast burritos, combination plates, salsas, and fresh house favorites.

How can I contact the restaurant?

Call (760) 341-3553 or visit the restaurant in Palm Desert.

Is Victor’s Mexican Food family-friendly?

Yes. Victor’s Mexican Food is a casual, family-friendly Mexican restaurant in Palm Desert with a welcoming atmosphere for locals, visitors, families, and groups.

Is Victor’s Mexican Food good for a quick lunch or dinner?

Yes. Victor’s is a great choice for a quick lunch, casual dinner, or takeout meal, with fresh Mexican favorites like tacos, burritos, combination plates, breakfast items, and house specialties.

Where is Victor’s Mexican Food located in Palm Desert?

Victor’s Mexican Food is located on CA-111 in Palm Desert near Deep Canyon Road, at 74600 CA-111 Suite F, Palm Desert, CA 92260.

Do you have parking?

Yes. Victor’s Mexican Food is located in a shopping center with convenient parking for dine-in, takeout, and online order pickup.

What are popular menu items at Victor’s Mexican Food?

Popular favorites include breakfast burritos, tacos, fish tacos, wet burritos, chile verde, carnitas, combination plates, fresh salsas, and classic Mexican comfort food.

Can I browse the menu before I visit?

Yes. You can browse the online menu before visiting, whether you plan to dine in, order takeout, or place an online order when ordering is available.

Is Victor’s Mexican Food locally owned?

Yes. Victor’s Mexican Food is a family-owned Palm Desert restaurant rooted in Victor’s Zacatecas heritage, fresh ingredients, and warm local hospitality.

Do you serve visitors staying in Palm Desert and the Coachella Valley?

Yes. Victor’s welcomes both locals and visitors looking for fresh, casual Mexican food in Palm Desert and the surrounding Coachella Valley area.
