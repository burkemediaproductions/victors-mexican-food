Our story

From El Malacate to Palm Desert.

Victor’s Mexican Food is a family-owned Palm Desert restaurant shaped by Zacatecas roots, fresh ingredients, warm hospitality, and a lifelong love for cooking.

The beginning

A childhood dream that never went away.

Victor’s story begins in El Malacate, a rural village on the border of Zacatecas and Jalisco. As a child, he saw a chef on television and told his mother he would become one someday.

That dream stayed with him through years of work, learning, patience, and persistence. Today, the restaurant carries the same values that shaped him: family, service, resilience, and food prepared with care.

Rooted in Family

From Zacatecas tradition to Palm Desert hospitality, Victor’s is built around the feeling of being welcomed and cared for.

Heritage

Where commerce, travel, and hope crossed paths.

Victor’s family story traces back to an old Camino Real route, where his grandfather operated a small store that served people passing through the village. That spirit of hospitality still guides the restaurant today.

Today

Fresh food, friendly service, and a local welcome.

At Victor’s Mexican Food, guests find satisfying Mexican classics, scratch-made flavor, and a family-owned restaurant experience in the heart of Palm Desert.

The journey

From a Camino Real store to a Palm Desert restaurant.

Victor’s story carries generations of hard work, family business, service, and patience. Long before Victor’s Mexican Food opened in Palm Desert, the family dream began in the Sierra, where his grandfather built a small store along an old Camino Real route.

“Hard work is the dream.” A value passed down through generations.
Generations ago

A small family store in the Sierra

Victor’s grandfather opened a store in a rural area near the Zacatecas and Jalisco border, serving arrieros traveling by horse along the Camino Real. They carried goods like cheese, sugar, cinnamon, flour, spices, and cloth between small communities and larger towns.

1993

Victor moved to the Coachella Valley

After coming to the United States, Victor made the Coachella Valley home. Over time, he built his life around work, family, cooking, and the community he continues to serve today.

2009

A promise became real

As a child, Victor saw a chef on television and told his mother he would become one someday. Years later, when he earned his first chef position, he sent her a photo wearing the chef hat and apron he once dreamed about.

December 15, 2018

Victor’s Mexican Food opened

After more than 25 years of patience and persistence, Victor opened his own restaurant, carrying forward the family values of hard work, hospitality, and business ownership.

May 17th, 2025

A new Palm Desert Location

Victor’s Mexican Food entered its next moving to a prime Palm Desert location, giving longtime guests and new visitors a welcoming place for fresh Mexican food, family service, and local flavor.

Beyond the kitchen

A local story that reaches beyond the dining room.

Victor’s Mexican Food is part of a larger Palm Desert story built on hospitality, family, and service. These two parts of Victor’s life help show how deeply he cares for people and for the Coachella Valley community.

Local involvement

Proud to be part of Palm Desert and the Coachella Valley.

Victor loves being part of the Palm Desert and Coachella Valley community. For local families, workers, and visitors, the restaurant is meant to feel welcoming, dependable, and full of care.

That sense of connection shapes the experience at Victor’s Mexican Food every day—from friendly service and familiar faces to fresh meals shared by neighbors, friends, and returning guests.

White Doves for You PS

A meaningful service rooted in care, remembrance, and celebration.

Outside the restaurant, Victor is also connected to White Doves for You PS, a Coachella Valley dove release service that brings beauty, peace, and meaning to special moments.

The service is part of weddings, memorials, Quinceañeras, celebrations, and other gatherings throughout the valley—another expression of the same thoughtfulness and respect that guests feel at Victor’s Mexican Food.
