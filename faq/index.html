FAQ

Helpful answers before you visit.

Find quick answers about ordering, dine-in, takeout, hours, location, and menu questions at Victor’s Mexican Food.

Good to know

Planning your visit is easy.

Whether you are dining in, picking up takeout, or browsing the menu online, Victor’s keeps the experience simple and welcoming.

Browse MenuGet Directions
Do you offer dine-in and takeout?

Yes. Victor’s Mexican Food welcomes dine-in guests and offers takeout for guests on the go.

Can I order online?

Yes. You can browse the live menu and order online when online ordering is available. When online ordering is closed, the menu remains available to browse.

Where are you located?

Victor’s Mexican Food is located at 74600 CA-111 Suite F, Palm Desert, CA 92260.

Do you serve breakfast?

Yes. Breakfast burritos and breakfast plates are available during morning hours.

What kinds of food are guests known to love?

Guests often mention tacos, burritos, fish tacos, chile verde, carnitas, chorizo breakfast burritos, combination plates, salsas, and fresh house favorites.

How can I contact the restaurant?

Call (760) 341-3553 or visit the restaurant in Palm Desert.
